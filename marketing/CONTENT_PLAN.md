# Build-in-public content plan

The pitch is the experiment, not the returns: "I had Claude build a real-money trading bot. Here is what it
actually does, wins and losses." Every number below is real as of Nov 25 2026; update them from the dashboard
before posting. Never promise profit, and put "not financial advice" in the caption.

## Clips (marketing/)
- clip_dial.mp4: the hero. "8,000 checks, 40 bets."
- clip_gates.mp4: the funnel of what gets rejected.
- clip_real.mp4: the real-money curve.
- clip_bets.mp4: the latest real bets, wins and losses.
- dashboard_scroll_vertical.mp4: the whole page.

## Hooks (first 2 seconds, on screen and spoken)
1. "My trading bot looked at 8,000 prices yesterday. It bet on 40."
2. "I let an AI trade my real money for two weeks. Here are the actual numbers."
3. "This bot lost six bets in a row today. Here's why I didn't turn it off."
4. "Everyone shows you the wins. Here's my bot's real profit: twenty-three dollars."
5. "I tested the 'smart' way to place orders on 2,000 real markets. It doesn't work."

## Scripts (15 to 25 seconds each)

**1. What a trading bot sees** (clip_dial, then clip_gates)
"Every minute this bot checks seven crypto prices on Kalshi against its own model. Yesterday that was about
eight thousand checks. It bet on forty. Most die here, where the model says the edge is too small. It's up
twenty-three dollars in two weeks on real money. Not life-changing. But every number on this screen is real."

**2. The honest scoreboard** (clip_real, clip_bets)
"Two weeks, five hundred thirty real bets, plus twenty-three dollars. That could still be luck, and I'll show
you when it isn't. Today it lost six in a row. Follow if you want to see whether this thing actually works."

**3. The thing that didn't work** (dashboard scroll)
"Everyone says: don't pay the asking price, leave a bid and wait. I replayed that against Kalshi's real trade
history. The bids that got filled lost. The ones that never filled were the winners. So the bot pays up."

**4. The fix** (clip_gates)
"One in four of my bot's orders were missing. The price moved in the one second before the order landed.
Now it re-checks the price the instant it sends. First ten orders after the fix: ten filled."

**5. Tennis** (any clip)
"My tennis bot went thirteen for thirteen. Then one loss wiped out nine wins. That's what betting favourites
at ninety cents looks like."

They are what makes the winning days believable.
