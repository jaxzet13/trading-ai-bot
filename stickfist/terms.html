What real money buys

  • Play money. Real money adds play money to your balance in the game: $1.00 of play money for every $1.00 you pay, in whole dollars from $1 to $25 an order. There are no bundles, bonuses or discounts. You spend the balance on packs at the price shown in the game, which moves with the card market.
  • Play money, the cards and every price in the game have no cash value. They can't be cashed out, sold for real money or transferred to anyone for real money. Nothing in the game pays out money.
  • Packs are random. What is in a pack is decided by chance. The exact odds are on the Odds page and don't change with what you have spent or pulled. Every card comes out of a pack at its set starting value. At those values a pack's cards sell back in the game for about 92% of the pack's price on average, and roughly 40 to 47% of packs sell back at least what they cost. After that, each card's value moves on its own (see below). That is play money and never becomes cash.
  • You must be 18 or older. Don't buy packs if you are not.
  • Spending limit. You can spend at most {{CAP}} a day. The game also limits how many packs you can open in a day and reminds you to take breaks. If you want to stop, stop: you owe nothing further.

Refunds

  • Play money you have added and not spent: refunded in full on request within 14 days of paying.
  • Play money you have already spent on packs can't be refunded, because opened packs can't be un-opened, unless something went wrong (you were charged twice, the money didn't arrive, or the game failed while you were opening a pack). In those cases we refund or replace it.
  • Refunds go back to the card or wallet you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
  • Ask at {{CONTACT}} with the time of the payment.

Fair play

  • Every digital pack is drawn from a secret seed that was locked in (as a hash) before you opened it. You can check afterwards that your packs followed the odds. See "Check your packs" on the Odds page.
  • After you pull a card, its value is set by simulated AI buyers (bots, not people) trading that one copy. Details of a copy affect how buyers value it, and on average a copy drifts a little down while you hold it. None of it involves other people's money.

Privacy

  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe shows us whether a payment succeeded.
  • What we keep for play money: a random player id (not your name), counters, the days you visited, a one-way hash of your network address (never the address), the pack seed, and for each purchase a Stripe session id and the amount.
  • What we don't keep: your name, email, card details or home address on our own server.
  • Your cards and balance are stored in your own browser. Clearing your browser data clears them.
  • We don't sell your data. Ask at {{CONTACT}} to have what we hold about you deleted (give us your player id from Me › Limits and settings), as far as the law allows.
