# Prompts for the Requiem paintings

The Figma-made prompts end with "Original design. No text, no letters, no watermark, no border, no frame." Style words come from the briefing pictures; no artist, brand or character is named.

Models: Pallbearer, Vigil, Halation, The Witness and Seraph were made with `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` (1024 × 1280, requested 4:5). Orrery was made with `gpt_image_2_6` (quality high, 2k, 4:5, 1792 × 2240) **with the briefing's pixel-cosmos picture attached as a style reference**; the other five were made from text alone. `spare-knight.jpg` is an unused alternative for Pallbearer.

## R1 Pallbearer (`pallbearer.jpg`)
Four hooded mourners in tattered grey robes carry a heavy closed coffin on their shoulders through a moonlit graveyard, a ghostly cold fog rolling between old headstones, bare trees, a broken stone angel in the foreground, a pale full moon behind thin cloud. Dark moody oil painting in monochrome blue-grey and black with one faint warm lantern glow, visible painterly brush texture, soft haze, delicate stippled light on the fog, fine film grain, heavy crushed shadows, solemn, atmospheric, extremely detailed.

## R2 Vigil (`vigil.jpg`)
A lone hooded figure holds up a glowing lantern and walks toward a colossal luminous angel wing that rises behind a Gothic church spire, rows of crooked tombstones in the foreground, cold fog drifting between them. Cyanotype print look: strictly monochrome Prussian blue and white, deep indigo shadows, pale blue-white highlights, visible paper grain and ink bleed, fine stipple and halftone shading, soft sensor grain, dreamy yet ominous, extremely detailed.

## R3 Halation (`halation.jpg`)
A faceless robed saint standing in a ruined cathedral nave with a thin ring halo floating above the head, arms hanging open, body half-dissolving into digital glitch. Shot as a corrupted VHS tape frame: strong RGB channel split with red, cyan and magenta ghost copies offset sideways, horizontal tracking-error bands, datamosh smears, scanlines, tape noise, chromatic aberration, and an iridescent oil-slick sheen of pink, green, violet and gold across the highlights. Dark murky background with shafts of dusty light, heavy film grain, moody, eerie, beautiful, extremely detailed.

## R4 The Witness (`witness.jpg`)
A colossal open human eye fills the night sky like a moon, iris and pupil rendered in intricate radial detail, its lower lashes dripping into thin rays of light, hanging over a vast silent graveyard of crooked headstones, iron fences and a gothic cathedral skyline far below. Black and white only. Rendered as a high-contrast linocut and stipple engraving: thousands of fine white dots and hatched lines on a black ground, coarse print grain, crisp ink edges, deep crushed blacks, the eye glowing with stippled light, a lone small cloaked figure standing among the graves for scale. Dark, mythic, haunting, extremely detailed.

## R5 Orrery (`orrery.jpg`)
Pixel-art cosmic illustration in the style of the reference image (pixel stair-steps, blue and white, deep navy, tiny stars, orbiting planets on thin lines), new original subject: a hooded skeletal king seated on a throne made of orbiting planets, a spiral galaxy where the crown should be, blue-white glow, pixelated edges. High detail. Portrait trading-card illustration. No text, no timestamp, no watermark, no border.

(The reference image was attached as an image reference. The subject, pose, staff and throne are new, but the palette, the pixel treatment and the galaxy-for-a-head idea come from it. Treat Orrery as the card with the closest tie to its reference and check it against the original before printing.)

## R6 Seraph (`seraph.jpg`)
A colossal armored archangel descending head-first through a vast ring of white fire into darkness, six enormous feathered wings spread across the whole frame, a long spear of pure light in one gauntleted hand, face hidden behind a smooth faceless helm, tattered cloth streaming upward. Violet and ice-blue anamorphic lens flares streak horizontally across the frame, cosmic dust and burning embers drift in the air. Rendered as a dark, grainy stipple and halftone illustration on a pure black ground: dense fine dotwork shading, coarse film grain, shapes built from white and pale-violet stippled light, deep crushed blacks, mythic, menacing, cathedral-scale, painterly, extremely detailed.

## Spare (`spare-knight.jpg`)
A lone armored pallbearer-knight kneeling in heavy rain, head bowed, dented plate armor and a tattered cloak, holding a small lit lantern in one gauntlet and a wreath of white daisies and lilies in the other, with a toppled stone angel and wet cobblestones around him. Painterly grayscale acrylic painting on a black ground: loose, visible brush strokes, white and silver paint streaks forming slanting rain across the whole canvas, soft mist, a single warm amber glow from the lantern as the only colour, deep crushed blacks, heavy paint texture and subtle film grain.
