# Funny commons

Idea: commons are goofy and unrewarding on purpose, so the rare cards feel crazy badass by contrast.
Every common is a stick figure who takes it completely seriously while wearing or holding something ridiculous.

| Card | Fighter | The bit | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stitch | boxing stance, frog hat | frog hat. zero regrets. |
| 2 | Brick | defensive crouch, trash-can-lid shield, colander helmet | it is a colander. it works. |
| 3 | Rook | lunge, floppy pool-noodle spear | reach: floppy. |

All at the Common tier: classic ASCII, phosphor green, 2,816 characters. The caption is the optional `caption=` argument in tools/ascii2.py
(small, dim, right-aligned on the copy-ID line). Base art: Figma GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst (sources/).
