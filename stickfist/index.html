Odds & rules

Card values

How pulls work

  • Each card in a pack rolls its rarity on its own, using the odds printed on the pack.
  • Within a rarity, every fighter is equally likely.
  • Rolls use your browser's cryptographic random generator. Odds never change based on what you've spent or pulled.
  • Every pull gets a serial number.

Coins, tokens, buyback

  • Coins cost $1.00 per 100. Bigger bundles add bonus coins.
  • Tokens are earned only by fighting. They can't be bought or sold back.
  • Foil cards sell back for 85% of card value. Sketch cards are valued at 10% of foil.
  • On average every pack returns less than it costs. Each pack shows exactly how much less.

Before real money

This demo can't take payments. A real launch would need at least:

  • An adult-owned business and a lawyer's sign-off on the pack and buyback model
  • ID checks so every buyer is 18+, before their first purchase
  • A payment processor that approves the model in writing
  • Spending limits and a self-exclusion option
