Card values

How pulls work

  • Each card in a pack rolls its rarity separately, using the odds printed on the pack.
  • Within a rarity, every fighter is equally likely.
  • Rolls use your browser's cryptographic random number generator. Nothing is weighted by how much you've spent.
  • Each pull gets a serial number, shown in the top corner of the card.

Buyback

  • Sell any fighter back for 85% of its card value, any time.
  • The rate is fixed. If it ever changed, it would be announced here first and never applied to cards you already own.
  • On average, packs return less than they cost. The "if you sell everything back" number on each pack shows how much less.

Before real money

This demo can't take payments. A real-money launch would need, at minimum:

  • An adult-owned business and a lawyer's sign-off on the pack and buyback model
  • ID checks so every buyer is 18+, before their first purchase
  • A payment processor that approves the model in writing
  • Physical cards in stock for every fighter that can be pulled
