Odds & terms

Rarity

Pulls

  • Every booster holds 5 cards: 2 commons, 1 uncommon, a reverse-foil slot (which can instead hold an Illustration or a Masterwork), and the rare slot. Each slot rolls with the odds printed on the pack.
  • Inside a rarity, every fighter is equally likely, except a pack's featured fighter, whose boost is printed on the pack.
  • Rolls use your browser's cryptographic random generator. Odds never change with what you've spent or pulled.

Currency

  • Balances and prices are shown in demo dollars. Larger deposits include a bonus.
  • Tokens come only from Arena fights. The winner is rolled from the two fighters' stats (win chance shown on each opponent), then the fight plays out. A win pays 10 to 100 tokens by opponent rarity; a loss pays 3.
  • Card values come from the simulated market: each card has a raw near-mint price that moves once per market day (15 minutes) with scarcity, fighter hype and news. Sell-back is 90% of today's price. Sketch cards are valued at 10% of foil.
  • Limited packs (Bloodmoon, Gold Scroll) hold 3 cards: a common, an uncommon and one limited-edition card (84% base, 14% Ultra, 2% super rare). Each has a print run of 5,000 packs sold over a short window and is never reprinted; once sold out its cards only trade on the market and their trend steepens.
  • Packs never sell below their launch price. When the cards inside appreciate, the pack price rises with them.
  • Every card is pulled with a hidden condition (centering, corners, edges, surface, 6–10). Ink Grading Co. reveals it for a fee and seals the card in a slab. Graded value is a multiple of raw: 10 is worth 1.6× (commons) to 6× (Masterwork), Black Label (four perfect 10s) doubles that, 9 is worth a little more than raw, 8 and below less.
  • Every pulled card gets a grade from 6 to 10. Grade multiplies the card's base value: 6 ×0.5 (18%), 7 ×0.75 (24%), 8 ×1 (26%), 9 ×1.5 (20%), 10 ×2.5 (12%).
  • Hit rate is the share of packs whose cards sell back for at least the pack price, from 3,000 simulated openings with the published odds.
  • Levels: every $1 spent on packs is 100 XP. Each new level pays a bonus of $0.25 plus $0.15 per level. One free First Ink pack every 24 hours.
  • Every pack returns less than it costs on average. Each pack prints exactly how much.

Before real money

  • An adult-owned business and a lawyer's sign-off on paid packs with cash sell-back
  • ID verification so every buyer is 18+, before their first purchase
  • A payment processor that approves the model in writing
  • Deposit limits and self-exclusion
