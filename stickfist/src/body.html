Movers
Every copy you hold has a value of its own. Simulated AI buyers trade each copy separately, so two copies of the same card can go opposite ways.

    

    

    

    
How values work
    
      
  • A card always comes out of the pack at the same value: its starting value, times its style if it has one.
    • 
      
  • From then on that copy is yours alone. Simulated AI buyers (bots, not people) trade it around the clock. Its value drifts, rushes up when buyers pile in, and drops when they dump it. Rushes fade over a few days.
    • 
      
  • Buyers aren't random. Small details of a copy make them keener or cooler, and each copy is judged on its own. Nobody tells you which details matter: that's the part you learn.
    • 
      
  • On average a copy drifts a little down while you hold it, so holding everything forever is not a plan. Some copies climb a long way. Selling pays 90% of the value.
    • 
      
  • All of this is play money with no cash value.
