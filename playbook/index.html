    6. From one account to many

    How the payouts grow. One signal copied to several accounts is one bet placed several times, so keep the sizing rule on every one of them.

    7. Rules that get payouts denied, and the honest odds

    • No hedging across accounts or firms. Opposite positions so one side passes is trade-matched and ends with the payout denied and the account closed.
    • Copying one signal to accounts you own is normally allowed. Copying to accounts in other people's names, or group copy-trading, usually is not. Read the firm's rule.
    • Respect allocation caps. Most firms cap your total funded size (often $200k to $400k). Beyond that, add a second firm.
    • News rules on funded CFD accounts. Standard FTMO accounts restrict trades around high-impact news; 09:30 ET is clear of the 08:30 releases, but check FOMC days.
    • No martingale, no grid, no tick scalping. They pass evaluations sometimes and are flagged at payout.
    • Keep the funded sizing. A funded $50k futures account dies at 2% risk. Cut to 0.25% the day it is funded.
