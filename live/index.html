The rules it follows, every session

  1. At 9:35am New York, look at the 9:30 five-minute candle. Closed above the 12 EMA: buy. Below: sell.
  2. Stop at twice the candle's size from the entry. Size the trade so the stop costs the whole remaining $2,000.
