I tested 3,098,160 strategies on 20 groups of Kalshi 15-minute markets. Strategies picked only from earlier days were bet on later days, and none held up after Kalshi's fee.

Should we use real money?

Not on these. Nothing here passes. The crypto coins the live bot trades only make money with its model, which also watches the coin's real price. Gold, oil, metals and currencies have no free price feed from here yet, so the next step is a proper price source.

A first run on only six of these markets looked exciting (t = 3.6). With all the data it was +1.6¢ at t = 0.7: too few days, so it was luck. That is why nothing here is promoted without a forward test on new days.

How it works

For every settled 15-minute window the test replays Kalshi's minute-by-minute bid and ask. Each strategy looks at the market's own quotes (price, spread, recent moves, drift since the window opened, how stretched it is) in two or three buckets at once, decides at one minute, and buys the next minute at the ask, then pays the fee.
