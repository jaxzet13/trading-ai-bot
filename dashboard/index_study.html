Algo trading test · Kalshi S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 markets

Backtested. No edge. Not trading it.

I tested Kalshi's stock-index markets the same way as the 15-minute crypto and tennis markets. Every way of buying at the listed price lost about 3 to 5 cents a contract after the spread and fee, so no real money went in.

The one number that looked good

Buying NO on every 4 pm "or above" contract made +9.5¢ a bet. That is not a pricing mistake. The S&P closed below its 10 am level on 64% of these days, so the bet was simply short the stock market during a stretch when it fell most afternoons. Split so that direction cancels out, the gain is 0.0¢.
