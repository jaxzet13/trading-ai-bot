How it works

For every settled game in the last 60 days the bot replays Kalshi's minute-by-minute prices. It decides on one minute's price and buys the next minute at the worst ask, so there are no lucky fills, then pays Kalshi's fee. Next up after this: same-game parlays, where the winner, spread and total of one game are linked and might be mispriced as a combo.
